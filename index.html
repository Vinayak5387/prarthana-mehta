About Prarthana Mehta

More Than Just Music.

“For me, music is a language of its own. Through the piano, I express emotions, tell stories, and create moments that stay with people long after the final note.”

— Prarthana Mehta

Trained at the Conservatoire National Supérieur de Musique de Paris and mentored by classical masters across Vienna, Élise developed an acoustic voice characterized by luminous clarity, harmonic poetry, and boundless emotional sincerity.

From solo concerts at the Philharmonie de Paris to the intimate halls of London and Tokyo, she connects generations of listeners to the visceral power of eighty-eight keys.

Her sonic landscape bridges French Impressionists like Maurice Ravel and Claude Debussy with the contemporary introspective worlds of Max Richter, Ryuichi Sakamoto, and Philip Glass.

The result is a dynamic fusion of pristine polyphonic control and ambient cinematic freedom that breathes new life into the concert stage.

Rather than viewing classical performance as a formal museum exhibit, Élise approaches every concert as a shared living sanctuary where audience and performer breathe together in real time.

She advocates for democratic access to acoustic music, open-door masterclasses for young artists, and acoustic recordings made without synthetic compression.
